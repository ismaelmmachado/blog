## Key Takeaways

1. **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** edges out GPT-4.5 overall, particularly in reasoning, creative tasks, and agent prompt design.

2. **GPT-4.5** maintains an advantage in knowledge-intensive tasks, code review, and translation fidelity.

3. Both models demonstrate strong performance across the board, with neither being "dominant" across all categories.

4. The **~0.5 point** margin is meaningful but not decisive — choose based on your specific use case.

## Recommendations

| Use Case | Recommended Model |

## Conclusion

The benchmark confirms Claude 3.5 Sonnet as the current top-tier model for most agentic and reasoning workloads, while GPT-4.5 excels in knowledge-heavy and translation tasks. For most AI agent building scenarios, Claude 3.5 Sonnet provides the better balance of capability and reliability.
